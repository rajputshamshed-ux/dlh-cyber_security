OPENING STATEMENT
-----------------
MedDefense has basic security defenses in place, but we have critical
weaknesses that put patient safety, financial stability, and our
reputation at risk. We are not prepared for a serious attack.

CRITICAL FINDING
----------------
Our MRI scanner is connected to our hospital network but runs on Windows XP
- software that has not received security updates since 2014. This is like
leaving the back door of your house unlocked with the key in the lock.
One attacker exploiting this vulnerability can move to our electronic
health records, billing systems, and patient monitoring devices.
Hospitals with this exact problem have lost $40 million in recovery costs
and delayed cancer treatments for a full week.

THREE PRIORITY ACTIONS
----------------------
1. IMMEDIATELY isolate the MRI and all medical devices on their own secure
   network segment. This costs $22,000 and can be done within one week.
   Without this, an attacker who gets in can reach equipment that keeps
   patients alive.

2. Require multi-factor authentication for all remote access and critical
   systems. This costs $8,000 and can be done within one month. Phished
   passwords will no longer be enough for attackers.

3. Deploy basic threat detection and an incident response plan.
   This costs $7,000 and can be done within one month. If an attack
   succeeds, we will know immediately and know what to do.

THE BUSINESS CASE
-----------------
The total cost for our top seven priority fixes is $42,000 - less than
one-tenth of the $5 million recovery cost hospitals have experienced
from similar breaches. This is not an expense. It is insurance against
a catastrophic event.

CLOSING
-------
If we do not act now, we are accepting the risk of a $40 million+ breach,
patient harm, regulatory fines, and a damaged reputation that could take
years to repair.
