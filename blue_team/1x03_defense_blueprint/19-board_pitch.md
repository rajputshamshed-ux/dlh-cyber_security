BOARD PITCH
================================================================================

CURRENT STATE
-------------
After four weeks of comprehensive security assessment, we have identified
31 vulnerabilities, 12 of which are critical. Our network is flat, meaning
any single compromised system can reach every other system, including
patient monitors and infusion pumps. We have no detection capability and
no incident response plan.

THE RISK
--------
If we do nothing, we face a ransomware attack that could encrypt our EHR
system, costing $4.3 million in breach response, fines, and lost revenue.
Three regional hospitals within 200 miles have already suffered this exact
scenario. The Change Healthcare breach paralyzed medical billing nationwide
for weeks. We are not immune.

THE PLAN
--------
We recommend deploying three foundational controls within the $120,000
budget: network segmentation to contain attacks ($12,000), MFA on all remote
access and administrative accounts ($8,000), and SIEM with daytime monitoring
for detection ($40,000). These controls disrupt 80% of known attack paths
and reduce our annual expected loss from $6.3 million to $1.4 million.

THE RETURN
----------
For every dollar invested, MedDefense will avoid $41 in expected losses. 
This is not an expense. It is insurance against a $4.3 million catastrophe.

We request approval to proceed immediately.
